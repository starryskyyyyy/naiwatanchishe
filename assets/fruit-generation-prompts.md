# 水果贴图生成记录

使用内置 imagegen。参考图为用户提供的黄色圆润小脸角色；苹果为红色、梨为黄色、桃子为粉色，均保留小绿眼睛和微笑，透明背景。

## apple

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ONE square transparent fruit sprite for a Snake game. Reference image is the identity/style guide: a plump smooth cute 3D character with two little green eyes, black pupils, a tiny horizontal smiling mouth and a soft rounded shape. Translate this face and rounded soft toy aesthetic into the specified fruit, preserving its recognizable fruit shape and fruit color. FRONT VIEW, eyes and mouth face the viewer, centered compact face on upper front, enough contrast to read at 24 pixels. No arms, legs, hands, clothing, labels, outlines, scenery, ground plane or cast shadow. Soft studio lighting from upper left. True transparent background, fully visible silhouette, fills 90% of square canvas with a small consistent margin. Single fruit, no sheet, no multiple versions. Fruit required: a RED APPLE: plump round apple silhouette with a gentle top cleft, short brown stem and one small green leaf. Red skin, green eyes.
